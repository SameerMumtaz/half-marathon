RULES.

Non-negotiables

EASY MEANS EASY

Conversational pace. If you can't talk in full sentences, slow down. 80% of running should feel embarrassingly slow — especially after lifting. The #1 mistake hybrid athletes make.

LEG DAY = MONDAY

Heavy squat/deadlift work goes on Monday only. That's 5 days clear of Saturday's long run. NEVER do heavy legs Thu/Fri — your long run will suffer and injury risk spikes.

LIFT FIRST, RUN AFTER

On combo days: lift first, fuel with carbs+protein, then run easy 30-60 min later. NEVER run hard after lifting — easy runs only. Hard runs (Thu) stay solo.

THE 10% RULE

Never increase weekly running mileage by more than 10-15%. Strength volume stays roughly flat. Don't ramp both at once. Injuries come from doing too much, too soon.

REST IS TRAINING

Sunday is FULL rest. Adaptation happens during rest, not workouts. Sleep 7-8 hrs. With 6 working days/week, recovery is your competitive advantage.

GET REAL SHOES

Luke's Locker or RunOn! in DFW for a gait analysis. Replace every 300-500 mi. Hybrid athletes pound joints harder — bad shoes will end your training.

DALLAS HEAT

Jun-Sep: lift in the gym (AC), run BEFORE 7am separately. Don't combo lift+run same session outdoors midday. Hydrate the day BEFORE long runs. Electrolytes past 60 min.

TAPER YOUR LIFTS TOO

W26-28: reduce lift volume ~30% (lighter weights or fewer sets). W29 race week: NO heavy lifts after Tuesday — light mobility/activation only. Goal: fresh legs Dec 13, not PRs the week before.

RACE DAY.

December 13 · execute the plan
  • 01Nothing new. Same shoes, socks, breakfast, gels you've trained with.
  • 02Lay everything out Saturday night. Bib pinned, gear stacked, alarm 2.5 hrs pre-race.
  • 03Eat 2-3 hrs before. 400-500 cal, low fiber, simple carbs. Oatmeal, toast w/ PB+honey, or banana + bagel.
  • 04Start SLOW. First 2 miles should feel comically easy. Almost everyone goes out too fast and pays after mile 8.
  • 05Fuel at miles 4, 8, 11. Gel or chews + water. Walking through water stations is fine — sip, don't gulp.
  • 06Mile 10 is the wall. Mentally, this is where it gets hard. Break the last 5K into one mile at a time. You've trained for this.
  • 07Finish smiling. Goal #1: finish. Goal #2: negative split. Goal #3 doesn't exist.
